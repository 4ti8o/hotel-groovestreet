About Hotel GrooveStreet Uganda

Discover our story of excellence in hospitality in the Pearl of Africa

Founded in 2010, Hotel GrooveStreet Uganda has established itself as a premier destination for luxury travelers seeking an exceptional experience in the heart of East Africa. Our commitment to excellence has made us a landmark of hospitality, combining contemporary elegance with warm, personalized service and authentic Ugandan hospitality.

Over 10 years of excellence in hospitality in Uganda
Committed to sustainable and responsible tourism in East Africa
Gateway to gorilla trekking, wildlife safaris, and cultural experiences
Team of internationally trained professionals with local expertise
